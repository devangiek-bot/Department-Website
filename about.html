About Our Department

Department of Commerce | Sri Venkateswara College

Welcome to the Department of Commerce

The Department of Commerce at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, provides students with opportunities to develop knowledge in commerce, business, finance and management.

The department focuses on academic learning, practical understanding and the development of professional skills. Students are encouraged to participate in academic and extracurricular activities.

Our Objectives

  • Develop strong foundations in commerce and business.
  • Encourage analytical and critical thinking.
  • Improve communication and professional skills.
  • Promote practical learning and teamwork.
  • Prepare students for higher education and careers.
